# Porting Reef_Ideal_A15 from COAWST v3.7 to v3.9

- **Date:** 2026-10-20 (port runs); image with all fixes pushed 2026-10-21
- **Status:** done

## Question / why

The author's idealized reef case was built for COAWST v3.7. Port it to v3.9
as-is (the first step of the plan), so it
can be benchmarked on Cloud and serve as the base of the ensembles.

## What was run

- `cases/Reef_Ideal_A15`: 203 x 111 rho points, N = 10, 6 km alongshore
  (east-west periodic) x 5 km; lagoon 10 m, one 10 m deep pass; 0.15 m
  12 h tide at the north boundary; SWAN JONSWAP Hs 1 m, Tp 10 s; T/S fixed.
- Changes from the v3.7 files: header unchanged; `ocean.in` with v3.9's
  YAML `varinfo`, `VEGNAM` renamed `VEGNAME`, new v3.9 keys appended;
  v3.9 `coupling.in` template; SWAN `BLOCK` output `.mat` -> `.nc`;
  `ana_fsobc.h` with A15's tide; `ana_m2obc.h` and the SCRIP weight file
  dropped (the SWAN grid is the ROMS grid).
- Port runs on the control VM (c3d-standard-8): 4 ROMS (2x2) + 4 SWAN.

## Results

- 1 simulated hour in 189 s. ROMS is the bottleneck (it waited only 1.7 s
  for SWAN), unlike Inlet_test.
- ROMS time per process: WEC kernel 46 %, 2-D (barotropic) kernel 32 %,
  GLS 7 %. The WEC share is mostly `wec_stokes.F`, recomputing the Stokes
  drift profile every 6 s step at every point and level, although the wave
  fields change only every 600 s exchange.
- v3.7 had only bulk Stokes drift; v3.9 defaults to spectral Stokes drift,
  so the port differs in physics from the author's v3.7 runs there.

## Decision / outcome

Port kept with A15's values. Image `v3.9-26e6fb7-reef_a15`
(`sha256:3b7e0961...`, non-periodic SWAN) pushed after
the SWAN plant-input fix; later replaced by the periodic image (2026-10-21).

## Open questions

- Recomputing the Stokes drift only after an exchange could save much of
  the WEC time but changes results slightly: open.
- Original A15 domain facts in the case pages in `docs/` were partly from a
  coarse profile; the crest depth was corrected on 2026-10-23.
