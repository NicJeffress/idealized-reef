# Grid convergence: the 20 m A15 grid under-resolves the breaking zone

- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Status:** done; the breaking-zone refinement recovers the 10 m answer at +16 % cost (update below)

## Question / why

Is the 20 m grid of Reef_Ideal_A15 converged? A 10 m twin (every cell halved,
`tools/reef/refine_a15_grid.py`, DT 3 s, NDTFAST 24) was run in two regimes.

## What was run

1. Dense canopy, triads off, the original defaults (phase 0, p0-base vs
   p0-grid10).
2. Density-1 canopy, triads on, the defaults of 2026-11-02 (AB3-AM4,
   implicit stress and canopy drag; 4-degree spread, 20 frequencies), s-base
   vs s-grid10.

24 h, metrics over hours 12-24; on the 10 m grid the regions are mapped to
the same positions (lagoon rows 10-50, offshore 189-209, crest v-row 65,
forereef 71-89).

## What it means

- **The 20 m grid is not converged**, by a consistent 15-16 % in the set-up
  and 10-20 % in the crest transport, in both regimes; much more than any
  time-step or SWAN setting.
- **The difference comes from one narrow strip, the breaking zone** at the
  top of the fore reef (1010-1060 m from the shore, 0.7-1.6 m deep), which
  is two or three 20 m cells wide. The waves lose the same energy there on
  both grids (dissipation +0.2 %), but the 10 m grid resolves how that
  momentum is handed to the water; everything shoreward follows (the lagoon
  sits a uniform 3-5 mm lower).
- **A k-epsilon caveat:** ROMS's turbulence closure works column by column
  and is hydrostatic, which is sound while cells are much wider than the
  water is deep. At 10 m that holds on the crest (20x) and reef flat (5x),
  not in the pass (1x) or on the fore reef (below 1x). Strict convergence by
  uniform refinement may not exist; the aim is to know the grid effect and
  resolve where it comes from.

## Update: refining the breaking zone only (same day)

`tools/reef/refine_a15_grid.py --band 850 1150 --factor 2` splits only the rows
between 850 and 1150 m from the shore (the crest, the breaking zone and the
top of the fore reef): 203 x 126 instead of 203 x 111, 10 m rows across the
breaking zone, DT 6 s kept (barotropic Courant 0.81; largest Cv in the runs
0.28-0.33). Same metrics, regions mapped by distance from the shore.

With the settings of the 10 m pair (density-1 canopy, 4-degree spread, 20
frequencies, no `TKE_WAVEDISS`):

| Metric | 20 m | breaking-zone grid | uniform 10 m |
|---|---|---|---|
| lagoon set-up | 18.2 mm | **15.4 mm** | 15.3 mm |
| crest transport | 563 m³/s | **522 m³/s** | 500 m³/s |
| pass transport | 690 m³/s | **630 m³/s** | 636 m³/s |
| crest jet p99 / max | 0.21 / 0.29 m/s | **0.20 / 0.30 m/s** | 0.20 / 0.30 m/s |
| pass jet max | 0.46 m/s | **0.43 m/s** | 0.43 m/s |
| surface/mean speed, forereef | 2.61 | **2.78** | 2.83 |
| breaking dissipation | 2227 W/m | 2216 W/m | 2231 W/m |
| wall s per simulated hour | 69 | **80 (+16 %)** | 551 (8x) |

The band grid recovers the 10 m answer: the set-up and pass transport
exactly, the jets and the crest shear within 1-2 %, two thirds of the
crest-transport gap, at 16 % more cost instead of 8x.

With today's defaults (30-degree spread, 34 frequencies, `TKE_WAVEDISS`), 20 m
against the band grid: set-up 12.5 against 10.8 mm (-14 %), crest transport
465 against 439 m³/s (-6 %), pass transport -7 %, jets -1 to -5 %, breaking
dissipation +0.5 %; wall time 109 against 128 s per simulated hour (+17 %).
The fore-reef surface/mean speed ratio drops from 1.82 to 0.83, but this
metric is ill-conditioned in the surf zone: 1030-1050 m from the shore the
flow is an undertow circulation (onshore at the surface, offshore at the bed)
with a depth mean of only 1-2.5 cm/s. What the finer rows change there is a
weaker surface current (1.6 against 3.5 cm/s at 1050 m) and a stronger
undertow at the bed (5.7 against 3.4 cm/s at 1030 m); seaward of 1080 m the
two grids agree within about 15 % at currents below 1 cm/s. From now on
surface and bottom speeds are reported instead of their ratio in the surf zone.

## Decision / outcome

Proposed, and now tested (above): refine across the shore only over the breaking zone (about 10 m
cells from 850 to 1150 m, everything else as now). That adds about 15 rows,
keeps DT 6 s (Courant numbers there stay small), and should cost about 20 %
more, against 8x for the uniform 10 m grid. If it reproduces the 10 m answer
it becomes the A15 production grid. Not yet tested with the defaults of
2026-11-03 (30-degree spread, 34 frequencies, `TKE_WAVEDISS`).
