# Grid convergence: the 20 m A15 grid under-resolves the breaking zone

- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Status:** done; a local cross-shore refinement is the proposed next step

## Question / why

Is the 20 m grid of Reef_Ideal_A15 converged? A 10 m twin (every cell halved,
`tools/reef/refine_a15_grid.py`, DT 3 s, NDTFAST 24) was run in two regimes.

## What was run

1. Dense canopy, triads off, the original defaults (phase 0, p0-base vs
   p0-grid10).
2. Density-1 canopy, triads on, the defaults of 2026-11-02 (AB3-AM4,
   implicit stress and canopy drag; 4-degree spread, 20 frequencies), s-base
   vs s-grid10.

24 h, metrics over hours 12-24; on the 10 m grid the regions are mapped to
the same positions (lagoon rows 10-50, offshore 189-209, crest v-row 65,
forereef 71-89).

## What it means

- **The 20 m grid is not converged**, by a consistent 15-16 % in the set-up
  and 10-20 % in the crest transport, in both regimes; much more than any
  time-step or SWAN setting.
- **The difference comes from one narrow strip, the breaking zone** at the
  top of the fore reef (1010-1060 m from the shore, 0.7-1.6 m deep), which
  is two or three 20 m cells wide. The waves lose the same energy there on
  both grids (dissipation +0.2 %), but the 10 m grid resolves how that
  momentum is handed to the water; everything shoreward follows (the lagoon
  sits a uniform 3-5 mm lower).
- **A k-epsilon caveat:** ROMS's turbulence closure works column by column
  and is hydrostatic, which is sound while cells are much wider than the
  water is deep. At 10 m that holds on the crest (20x) and reef flat (5x),
  not in the pass (1x) or on the fore reef (below 1x). Strict convergence by
  uniform refinement may not exist; the aim is to know the grid effect and
  resolve where it comes from.

## Decision / outcome

Proposed: refine across the shore only over the breaking zone (about 10 m
cells from 850 to 1150 m, everything else as now). That adds about 15 rows,
keeps DT 6 s (Courant numbers there stay small), and should cost about 20 %
more, against 8x for the uniform 10 m grid. If it reproduces the 10 m answer
it becomes the A15 production grid. Not yet tested with the defaults of
2026-11-03 (30-degree spread, 34 frequencies, `TKE_WAVEDISS`).
